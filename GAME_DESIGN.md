# Game design — planned after fitness foundation

Original dark-fantasy training world. Locations: Crimson Forge (Push), Titan Arena (Lower), Abyss Citadel (Pull), Ascension Tower (Full Body). Names and visual designs require normal originality review before commercial release. No copied anime characters, iconography, music or lore. Procedural developer assets are acceptable and clearly tracked.

Hunter XP, Gold and cosmetics come from legitimate prescribed training and recovery. Extra sets do not inflate boss credit or base reward. Cosmetic equipment does not make claims about physical strength. Fantasy Power/Endurance/Discipline/Control/Consistency explain their behavioral inputs and are separate from fitness metrics.

Proposed balancing defaults: XP to next level = 100 + 25 × (level−1) + 5 × (level−1)²; levels begin at 1. Store cumulative XP; use integer checked arithmetic and a bounded level range. Scheduled completed workout grants 100 XP/25 Gold, qualifying partial session at most 40 XP/10 Gold with no completion loot, recovery objective 20 XP once per occurrence. These are testable design assumptions, not implemented economy claims. PR bonus capped per occurrence and never scales with added volume.

Ranks E/D/C/B/A/S/S+ require explicit level, completed-session, adherence, PR and trial criteria from a versioned table. Proposed table must be finalized in M5 before implementation; no rank derives from load lifted alone. Achievement/skill trees: Discipline, Strength, Control, Endurance, Recovery. Cosmetic unlocks can be earned through recovery; no paid Recovery Tokens and no punishment for illness/travel.

Boss HP fraction = 1 − credited prescribed work / planned prescribed work, clamped. Skipped work does not falsely defeat the boss; partial completion can end safely with partial rewards. Replaying animation or editing history cannot add damage. No twitch controls during lifting. Short optional attack → recovery; save precedes both. Weekly dungeon tracks four distinct scheduled occurrences, never four duplicate workout uploads.

Celebrations: workout results first; level/rank/loot sequences skippable and Reduce Motion aware. Audio original/licensed and optional. No paid randomized loot, stat claims, public competitive anti-cheat promises or pressure to train through pain.
